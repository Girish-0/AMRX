## Evidence and document control

- [Status](STATUS.md) records available and missing evidence.
- [Decisions](decisions/README.md) explain major choices.
- [Validation records](validation/README.md) define how results must be recorded.
- [Provenance](provenance/README.md) maps all 13 ZIP entries to 12 unique artifacts and records the existing v2 source.

Original research is preserved byte-for-byte. Citation numbers, chat citation tokens, and embedded source claims in those files may be incomplete or stale. They are not silently repaired or presented as independently verified. Use the maintained guides for navigation and add sourced corrections through a PR.
