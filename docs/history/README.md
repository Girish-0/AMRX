## What changed after the audit

The v2 roadmap withdraws the inherited 100 kg/carbon-fibre configuration, a universal one-tonne envelope, fixed clamp preload and synchronized wedge arrangement. It also withdraws unsubstantiated mass, stiffness, scanner-angle, fatigue-life, wear, manufacturing and commercial guarantees. Read v2 §1.3 for the exact list and §15 for the F01–F65 dispositions.

The current direction favours one bounded configuration, a supported power-isolated exchange, manually operated positive retention, and a passive second module. Design responses address the audit on paper; they do not experimentally close its findings.

## How to read the archive

Names such as “final”, “validated” and “design freeze” are original source titles. Their claims retain historical context and may conflict with the later baseline. Historical files are immutable evidence snapshots; put new conclusions in maintained documents or a new revision and link back to the source.
