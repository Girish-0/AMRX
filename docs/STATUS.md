## What the numbers mean

The v2 baseline's 250 kg top-load and 50 kg conveyor cargo are **targets with different mass boundaries**. The base envelope, interface spacing, material choices and clamp arrangement are proposed parameters. No number becomes a tested result merely because it appears in a slide, calculation or roadmap.

Use these labels in new work: **requirement**, **proposal**, **target**, **assumption**, **calculated example**, or **verified result**. A verified result must name its configuration, method, raw evidence, acceptance criterion and reviewer.
